APOBANGPO EXHIBITION

The Forever Archive

In the spring of 2013, seven boys from a small company in Seoul stepped onto a stage with nothing but a dream, a song called "No More Dream," and a future they could not yet see.

They probably didn't know it then, but that small beginning would become a story that reached millions of hearts across the world.

And I was one of those people who grew up watching that story unfold.

I found BTS when I was young, and somehow, along the way, their music became a part of my own growing-up years. I watched them grow from seven boys chasing a dream into artists who stood on some of the biggest stages in the world. I listened to their songs through different chapters of my life — through happy moments, difficult days, lonely nights, and moments when I simply needed something familiar to hold onto.

For me, BTS has never been just about the records they broke or the stages they conquered. It's about the memories attached to their songs. It's about the seven people who reminded so many of us to dream, to keep going, to love ourselves, and to believe that our own story is worth telling.

So I created this archive as a small place to preserve that journey.

A place for the songs we grew up with.
The moments we will never forget.
The milestones we celebrated together.
And the memories that belong not only to BTS , but to ARMY too.

If you are visiting this archive as an ARMY, I hope you find something here that brings back a memory — a song that takes you to a certain year, a performance that made you fall in love with them all over again, or simply a moment that reminds you why you stayed.

Welcome, ARMY.
Let's remember the journey together.
